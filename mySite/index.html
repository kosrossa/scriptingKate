Kateryna Rossa

Kateryna Rossa is a full-time student in the Multimedia Production diploma. She is a first-year student at Lethbridge Polytechnic. Currently her favourite hobby is drawing and photography. She wants to learn more and gain experience in programs like Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator. She graduated from an Art School in Ukraine where her favourite activity was to make quick sketches of people posing. Then she started to study at the Odesa State Academy of Civil Engineering and Architecture. Kateryna did really well in a class called “Types of Fonts.” She participated in a school competition and won an award in the nomination called “Old Slavic Fonts.” Outside of school she likes to listen to different types of music like rock, alternative rock, lo-fi music, country music and so on. Kateryna also enjoys hiking. She was doing sports for a several years like swimming and triathlon. She won a lot of first places and set lots of records at a local swimming sport school.

About Me

My name is Kateryna Rossa, but I go by Kate. I'm originally from Odessa, Ukraine. It is a beautiful city near the sea. I graduated from high school and started university back home, but quit during my third year because I figured out that it was not really my thing and I also moved to Canada at that time. Before that, I also did sports such as swimming. As far as I remember, I started to swim since I was seven or eight years old. I was swimming for almost seven years. I also did triathlon for a couple years. At the same time with sports I was studying in an Art School and graduated after. That is why I dropped sports actually. Because I decided to continue pursuing creative work. And so, after I got accepted in the local university to study architecture. As I mentioned above, I had to quit after a while because there's a war started in my country, and me with my sister Nika moved to Canada in 2022. After that, we stayed in Calgary for a year where I went to English Classes to improve my English skills. Then we went to Fairmont Hot Springs which is in British Columbia. I was working there for almost three years to save up some money for studying. At that time, my sister graduated from high school in Invermere, small town near Fairmont Hot Springs, and got accepted in the University of Lethbridge to study for physics degree. And we decided to move here, in Lethbridge, together and continue our education.
